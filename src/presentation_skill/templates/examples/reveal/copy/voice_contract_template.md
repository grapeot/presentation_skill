# Voice contract

## Who is speaking, to whom
A concrete, scene-level description of the speaker's register (who they are, how they sound, what they refuse
to do), and the audience's situation.

## Speaker notes
- Podium voice: a person, an action, a consequence, then the idea.
- Sentences easy to say in one breath (most 8–18 words; none over 22). Natural contractions.
- Each slide's notes end by raising the question the next slide answers. No recap endings.
- Say why a list matters and how many items it has before listing.
- Banned: slogans, rhetorical triplets, "not X, but Y" more than twice, hype words.

## On-screen copy
- Short declarative phrases; every slot carries information; numbers only as given in the source contract.
