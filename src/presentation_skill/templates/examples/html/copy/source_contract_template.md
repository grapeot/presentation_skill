# Source contract

The only facts the writer may use. Keep every qualifier. The writer must not invent examples, anecdotes,
statistics, dates, company names or quotes.

## The event
- Who is speaking, to whom, when, for how long, and what the audience already knows.

## Numbers (each with its source and date)
- Number — what it measures, who measured it, when. Qualifier: what it does NOT mean.

## The speaker's positions (keep the nuance)
- Position — the exact boundary ("don't start with X", not "never use X").

## Stories the speaker owns
- Story — what happened, and what is illustrative rather than witnessed. Mark hypotheticals as hypotheticals.
